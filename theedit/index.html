01 / From the desk of the editor

I had a few stories to tell, some things on my mind, and questions that deserved better than getting lost in a text thread.

So here we are. A magazine. Which is probably a slightly dramatic solution, but at least this way the typography looks nice.

There'll be little updates from my life, the occasional discovery, things I've been listening to, and some questions if you're in the mood to answer them. Nothing serious and definitely no homework.

Consider this the first edition of a publication that takes itself just seriously enough.

— Pedro
